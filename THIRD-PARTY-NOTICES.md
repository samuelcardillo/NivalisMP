# Third-party components

The MIT license covers the original NivalisMP code in this repository. It does
not grant rights to the game, its artwork, generated game bindings, or other
third-party software.

The mod uses separately installed software:

- Nivalis Nights and its Unity runtime: supplied by the game publisher.
- [BepInEx](https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx), including its IL2CPP support and
  dependencies: installed separately under their respective licenses.
- [Harmony](https://github.com/pardeike/Harmony) and
  [Il2CppInterop](https://github.com/BepInEx/Il2CppInterop): provided by the
  compatible BepInEx installation.
- [.NET](https://github.com/dotnet/runtime) and
  [PowerShell](https://github.com/PowerShell/PowerShell): installed separately.

Game assemblies, generated interop assemblies, game assets, BepInEx, and .NET
binaries are not distributed in the repository or the mod release ZIP.
